# Compensation and Benefits Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-004
---
# Compensation Philosophy
Othellow Digital seeks to provide
competitive and sustainable compensation.
---
# Payroll
Payroll is processed twice monthly.
Payroll dates:
- 15th
- 30th
If payroll dates fall on a holiday,
payment shall occur on the preceding business day.
---
# Salary Adjustments
Adjustments may occur through:
- Merit increases
- Promotions
- Market adjustments
Salary adjustments are not guaranteed.
---
# Overtime
Overtime must receive manager approval.
Unauthorized overtime may not be compensated.
---
# Leave Benefits
## Vacation Leave
15 days annually
## Sick Leave
15 days annually
Unused leave treatment follows
applicable company guidelines.
---
# Bereavement Leave
Employees may receive
three (3) paid days per qualifying event.
---
# Parental Leave
Benefits shall comply with
Philippine labor regulations.
---
# Internet Allowance
Remote employees may receive
monthly internet reimbursement.
---
# Equipment Allowance
Certain roles may qualify
for approved equipment subsidies.
---
# Bonuses
Bonus eligibility depends on:
- Company performance
- Individual performance
- Budget availability
