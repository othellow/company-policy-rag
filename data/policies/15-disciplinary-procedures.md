# Disciplinary Procedures Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-015
Owner: Human Resources
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To establish fair and consistent procedures
for addressing policy violations.
---
# Guiding Principles
The disciplinary process shall be:
- Fair
- Consistent
- Documented
- Respectful
---
# Common Violations
Examples include:
- Attendance violations
- Security violations
- Harassment
- Insubordination
- Misuse of company resources
- Confidentiality breaches
---
# Informal Coaching
Managers should address minor concerns
through coaching whenever appropriate.
---
# Progressive Discipline
The company generally follows:
1. Verbal Warning
2. Written Warning
3. Final Written Warning
4. Termination
The company may skip steps
depending on severity.
---
# Investigation Process
Investigations may include:
- Interviews
- Evidence review
- System log review
- Witness statements
---
# Employee Cooperation
Employees are expected to cooperate
with legitimate investigations.
---
# Administrative Leave
Employees may be placed on administrative leave
during investigations when necessary.
---
# Serious Misconduct
Examples include:
- Fraud
- Theft
- Violence
- Major security violations
- Deliberate disclosure of confidential information
Serious misconduct may result
in immediate termination.
Refer to:
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
---
# Appeals
Employees may submit written appeals
within five business days of receiving
disciplinary decisions.
---
# Recordkeeping
Human Resources shall maintain
disciplinary records in accordance
with retention requirements.
Refer to:
OD-POL-013 Data Privacy Policy
