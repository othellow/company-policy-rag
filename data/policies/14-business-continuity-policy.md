# Business Continuity Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-014
Owner: Operations Department
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To ensure continued business operations
during disruptions or emergencies.
---
# Objectives
This policy seeks to:
- Protect employees
- Protect assets
- Maintain operations
- Minimize downtime
---
# Covered Events
Examples include:
- Natural disasters
- Cybersecurity incidents
- Extended power outages
- Internet disruptions
- Pandemic events
- Cloud service failures
---
# Recovery Priorities
Priority 1:
- Email Systems
- Collaboration Platforms
- Publishing Systems
Priority 2:
- Internal Reporting Systems
Priority 3:
- Non-Critical Applications
---
# Recovery Targets
Critical Services:
Recovery Time Objective (RTO):
8 Hours
Recovery Point Objective (RPO):
4 Hours
---
# Emergency Response Team
The team includes:
- CEO
- CTO
- CFO
- CHR
- CAI
---
# Communication Procedures
During emergencies:
- Employees shall follow official instructions
- Only authorized representatives may communicate externally
---
# Backup Requirements
Critical systems shall maintain:
- Automated backups
- Offsite storage
- Periodic recovery testing
---
# Testing Requirements
Business continuity exercises
shall be conducted annually.
---
# Post-Incident Review
Major incidents shall undergo
formal review and documentation.
