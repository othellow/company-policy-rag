# Marketing and Brand Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-009
Owner: Marketing Department
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
This policy establishes standards for brand management,
marketing activities, and public communications.
---
# Brand Principles
The Othellow Digital brand shall be:
- Educational
- Trustworthy
- Practical
- Helpful
- Professional
---
# Brand Voice
Content should be:
- Clear
- Respectful
- Informative
- Audience-focused
The use of misleading or deceptive language is prohibited.
---
# Logo Usage
Only approved versions of company logos may be used.
Modifications including:
- Color changes
- Distortion
- Cropping
- Unauthorized overlays
are prohibited.
---
# Marketing Campaign Approval
Campaigns below ₱50,000 require:
- Marketing Manager Approval
Campaigns from ₱50,000 to ₱250,000 require:
- Marketing Manager Approval
- Department Head Approval
Campaigns above ₱250,000 require:
- Marketing Manager Approval
- CFO Approval
- CEO Approval
Refer to:
OD-POL-011 Finance and Procurement Policy
---
# Social Media Accounts
Official accounts may only be created
with Marketing Department approval.
Account ownership belongs to the company.
---
# Public Statements
Only authorized spokespersons may:
- Speak to media
- Issue public statements
- Respond to journalists
---
# Influencer Partnerships
Partnerships must undergo:
- Brand review
- Legal review (if applicable)
- Financial review
---
# Advertising Standards
Marketing materials shall not:
- Contain false claims
- Misrepresent products
- Use deceptive practices
---
# AI-Generated Marketing Content
AI-generated marketing materials
must be reviewed by a Marketing Manager
before publication.
Refer to:
OD-POL-008 AI Governance Policy
---
# Record Retention
Campaign records shall be retained
for a minimum of three years.
