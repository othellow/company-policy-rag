# AI Governance Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-008
Owner: Chief AI Officer
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To establish responsible and secure
use of Artificial Intelligence technologies.
---
# Approved AI Platforms
The following platforms are approved:
- ChatGPT Enterprise
- Claude Enterprise
- Microsoft Copilot
- Gemini Enterprise
Other tools require CAI approval.
---
# Permitted Use Cases
Employees may use AI for:
- Research assistance
- Content ideation
- Draft creation
- Coding assistance
- Process automation
---
# Human Accountability
Humans remain responsible for:
- Final outputs
- Published content
- Customer communications
- Compliance obligations
AI may assist but may not replace accountability.
---
# Prohibited Activities
Employees shall not:
- Upload restricted data
- Upload customer PII
- Upload financial records
- Upload credentials
- Upload confidential contracts
unless explicitly authorized.
Refer to:
OD-POL-013 Data Privacy Policy
---
# AI Content Review
AI-generated content must undergo review.
Required reviewers:
Educational Content:
Editor + Subject Matter Reviewer
Marketing Content:
Marketing Manager
Technical Content:
Technical Reviewer
Legal Content:
Executive Approval Required
---
# Hallucination Prevention
Employees shall verify:
- Statistics
- Legal claims
- Medical claims
- Financial claims
before publication.
---
# AI Disclosure
Internal documentation shall identify
significant AI-generated contributions.
External disclosure may be required
based on legal and contractual obligations.
---
# AI Procurement
New AI tools require:
- Business Justification
- Security Review
- Privacy Review
- CAI Approval
---
# AI Experiments
Pilot projects shall:
- Define objectives
- Define risks
- Define success metrics
- Document outcomes
---
# Policy Violations
Violations may result in:
- Loss of AI access
- Disciplinary action
- Termination
