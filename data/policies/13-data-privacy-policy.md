# Remote Work Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-012
Owner: Human Resources
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To establish expectations for employees
working remotely or in hybrid arrangements.
---
# Eligibility
Remote work eligibility depends upon:
- Job responsibilities
- Performance history
- Business requirements
---
# Work Hours
Employees shall be available during
core business hours:
9:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Philippine Time
unless otherwise approved.
---
# Attendance Expectations
Employees shall:
- Attend required meetings
- Respond to communications promptly
- Maintain accurate schedules
---
# Home Workspace Requirements
Remote workspaces shall:
- Support productive work
- Protect confidential information
- Meet security requirements
---
# Security Requirements
Employees shall:
- Use company-approved devices
- Enable MFA
- Use secure networks
Public Wi-Fi shall not be used
without approved VPN protection.
Refer to:
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
---
# Equipment
Company-issued equipment remains
the property of Othellow Digital.
---
# Internet Connectivity
Employees are responsible for maintaining
reliable internet service.
Internet reimbursement may be available.
Refer to:
OD-POL-004 Compensation and Benefits Policy
---
# Performance Expectations
Remote employees are held to the same
performance standards as office-based employees.
---
# Remote Work Revocation
The company may revoke remote work privileges
for business, performance, or security reasons.
