# Operations Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-005
---
# Purpose
To ensure consistent execution of company projects
and operational activities.
---
# Project Lifecycle
Projects shall follow:
1. Initiation
2. Planning
3. Execution
4. Monitoring
5. Closure
---
# Project Ownership
Every project must have:
- Sponsor
- Project Owner
- Team Members
---
# Weekly Reporting
Department managers shall submit:
- Accomplishments
- Risks
- Issues
- Upcoming priorities
every Friday.
---
# Escalation Framework
Operational issues shall be categorized as:
## Low
Minimal impact.
Resolution target:
Five business days.
## Medium
Moderate impact.
Resolution target:
Two business days.
## High
Significant impact.
Resolution target:
One business day.
## Critical
Business disruption.
Immediate escalation required.
---
# Meeting Standards
Meetings shall include:
- Agenda
- Notes
- Action Items
- Owner Assignments
---
# Key Performance Indicators
Departments shall maintain
measurable performance metrics.
Examples include:
- Audience Growth
- Content Production Volume
- System Availability
- Budget Compliance
---
# Continuous Improvement
Managers shall identify
process improvement opportunities
on a quarterly basis.
