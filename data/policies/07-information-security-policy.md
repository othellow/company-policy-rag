# Information Security Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-007
Owner: CTO and Information Security Office
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To protect company information assets
from unauthorized access, disclosure,
modification, or destruction.
---
# Security Principles
The company follows:
- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability
(CIA Security Model)
---
# Security Responsibilities
All employees are responsible for:
- Protecting credentials
- Reporting incidents
- Following security procedures
---
# Password Standards
Passwords shall:
- Be at least 14 characters
- Contain multiple character types
- Not be reused across systems
Passwords shall never be shared.
---
# Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for:
- Email systems
- Administrative systems
- Cloud platforms
- Financial systems
---
# Access Management
Access shall follow
the Principle of Least Privilege.
Employees receive only the minimum access
required to perform assigned duties.
---
# Joiner-Mover-Leaver Process
## Joiners
Access shall be approved by:
- Hiring Manager
- System Owner
## Movers
Role changes require access review.
## Leavers
Access shall be revoked immediately.
---
# Security Incident Reporting
Employees shall report:
- Phishing attempts
- Malware infections
- Unauthorized access
- Data leaks
- Lost devices
within one hour of discovery.
---
# Incident Severity
## Critical
Business-wide impact
Response Target:
15 minutes
## High
Department-wide impact
Response Target:
1 hour
## Medium
Limited operational impact
Response Target:
4 hours
## Low
Minor security concerns
Response Target:
1 business day
---
# Security Monitoring
The company reserves the right to monitor:
- Email activity
- Device activity
- Network traffic
- Authentication events
for security purposes.
---
# Vendor Security
Third-party vendors must undergo
security review before onboarding.
Required:
- Security questionnaire
- Risk assessment
- Contract review
---
# Annual Security Training
Employees shall complete
mandatory security awareness training annually.
Failure to complete training
may result in suspension of system access.
