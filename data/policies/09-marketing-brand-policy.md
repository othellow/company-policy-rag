# Data Privacy Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-013
Owner: Information Security Office
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To ensure compliance with applicable
privacy and data protection laws.
---
# Data Classification
## Public
Information approved for public release.
Examples:
- Published content
- Marketing materials
---
## Internal
Business information intended
for internal use only.
Examples:
- Team reports
- Internal procedures
---
## Confidential
Sensitive business information.
Examples:
- Financial reports
- Vendor contracts
- Personnel records
---
## Restricted
Highest sensitivity classification.
Examples:
- Authentication credentials
- Government IDs
- Customer personal information
- Security keys
---
# Collection Principles
The company shall collect only data that is:
- Necessary
- Relevant
- Lawful
---
# Personal Data
Examples include:
- Names
- Addresses
- Contact details
- Government identifiers
---
# Access Controls
Access to personal data shall be limited
to authorized personnel.
---
# Data Retention
Data shall be retained only as long
as necessary for business or legal purposes.
---
# Data Disposal
Approved methods include:
- Secure deletion
- Cryptographic destruction
- Physical destruction
---
# Data Breach Reporting
Potential privacy breaches shall be reported
immediately to Information Security.
Target reporting time:
Within one hour of discovery.
---
# Third-Party Sharing
Third parties must:
- Sign confidentiality agreements
- Meet security requirements
- Demonstrate legal compliance
---
# Privacy Training
Employees handling personal information
shall complete annual privacy training.
---
# Cross-Border Transfers
Cross-border transfers require:
- Privacy review
- Security review
- Executive approval
