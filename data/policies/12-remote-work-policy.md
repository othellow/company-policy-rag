# Content Production Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-010
Owner: Content Production Department
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To establish standards for content creation,
quality assurance, and publishing.
---
# Content Categories
The company produces:
- Educational Content
- Lifehack Content
- Useful Entertainment Content
---
# Production Workflow
All content shall follow:
1. Research
2. Concept Approval
3. Draft Creation
4. Review
5. Editing
6. Publication
---
# Research Standards
Research shall prioritize:
- Credible sources
- Official publications
- Academic references
- Primary sources
---
# Fact Verification
Claims involving:
- Health
- Finance
- Law
- Statistics
must be independently verified.
---
# Content Review Requirements
Educational Content:
- Writer Review
- Editor Review
- Subject Matter Review
Technical Content:
- Technical Reviewer Approval
AI-Assisted Content:
- Human Review Required
Refer to:
OD-POL-008 AI Governance Policy
---
# Copyright Compliance
Employees shall not:
- Copy copyrighted content
- Use unauthorized images
- Republish protected materials
without authorization.
---
# Content Ownership
All content produced during employment
belongs to Othellow Digital.
---
# Content Corrections
Published errors shall be corrected promptly.
Major factual errors shall be escalated
to Content Leadership.
---
# Publishing Authority
Only authorized personnel may publish
to company-owned channels.
---
# Sensitive Topics
Content involving:
- Politics
- Health
- Finance
- Legal Advice
requires additional editorial review.
---
# Record Retention
Published content archives shall be retained
for a minimum of five years.
