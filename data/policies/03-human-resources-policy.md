# Human Resources Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-003
Owner: Human Resources
---
# Recruitment
## Job Requisition
Managers seeking additional staff must submit
a workforce request to Human Resources.
Approval requirements:
- Department Head
- CHR
- CFO (if budget impact exists)
---
## Candidate Screening
The recruitment process includes:
1. Resume Review
2. Initial Interview
3. Technical Assessment
4. Final Interview
5. Reference Check
---
## Employment Offers
Employment offers require:
- Department Head approval
- CHR approval
Director-level positions require CEO approval.
---
# Onboarding
New employees shall complete:
- Employment documentation
- Security awareness training
- Technology onboarding
- Policy acknowledgment
---
# Probationary Employment
New employees shall undergo a six-month probationary period.
Performance reviews shall occur at:
- Month 3
- Month 6
---
# Performance Management
Employees receive:
- Quarterly Check-ins
- Annual Performance Review
Evaluation criteria include:
- Results
- Collaboration
- Accountability
- Innovation
---
# Promotions
Promotions may be considered when:
- Performance exceeds expectations
- Required competencies are demonstrated
- Budget is available
---
# Learning and Development
Employees receive a yearly training allowance.
Managers shall maintain development plans
for direct reports.
---
# Employee Separation
Separation may occur through:
- Resignation
- Retirement
- Termination
- Redundancy
Exit interviews are encouraged.
---
# Offboarding
The following shall occur before separation:
- Asset return
- Account deactivation
- Knowledge transfer
- Final payroll review
