
# Othellow Digital
## Company Overview Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Executive Office
---
# Purpose
This document establishes the mission, vision, governance structure,
leadership responsibilities, and operating principles of Othellow Digital.
---
# Company Background
Othellow Digital is a digital media company focused on producing
educational content, practical lifehacks, and useful entertainment
for audiences across web, social media, streaming, and emerging
digital platforms.
The organization operates as a hybrid-first company employing
approximately forty-five (45) full-time employees.
---
# Mission
To make useful knowledge accessible, practical, and engaging.
---
# Vision
To become Southeast Asia's most trusted creator of educational
and life-improvement content.
---
# Core Values
## Curiosity
Employees are expected to maintain a learning mindset and actively
seek improvements in processes, products, and content.
## Integrity
Employees shall communicate honestly and comply with all
legal and ethical requirements.
## Accountability
Employees are responsible for outcomes, commitments,
and deadlines.
## Innovation
Employees are encouraged to responsibly leverage
technology and artificial intelligence.
## Service
All work should ultimately create value for audiences,
partners, and fellow employees.
---
# Organizational Structure
## Executive Office
Responsible for strategy, governance, and corporate direction.
## Human Resources
Responsible for employee lifecycle management.
## Finance
Responsible for budgeting, accounting, procurement,
and financial reporting.
## Technology
Responsible for systems, applications, and infrastructure.
## Information Security
Responsible for cybersecurity and risk management.
## Artificial Intelligence Office
Responsible for AI governance and standards.
## Marketing
Responsible for audience growth and brand awareness.
## Content Production
Responsible for research, writing, editing, and publishing.
## Operations
Responsible for coordination and execution.
---
# Decision-Making Authority
CEO approval is required for:
- Strategic initiatives
- Annual budgets
- Organizational restructuring
- Contracts exceeding ₱1,000,000
Department heads may approve routine operational decisions
within their authorized budgets.
---
# Communication Principles
Employees shall:
- Assume positive intent
- Communicate respectfully
- Escalate issues early
- Share relevant information promptly
---
# Document Ownership
The Executive Office shall review this policy annually.
