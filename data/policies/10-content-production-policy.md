
# Finance and Procurement Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-011
Owner: Chief Financial Officer
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
To ensure proper stewardship
of company financial resources.
---
# Budget Ownership
Each department head is responsible for
managing approved budgets.
---
# Procurement Thresholds
## Purchases up to ₱10,000
Manager Approval
---
## ₱10,001 - ₱50,000
Manager Approval
+
Department Head Approval
---
## ₱50,001 - ₱250,000
Department Head Approval
+
CFO Approval
---
## Above ₱250,000
Department Head Approval
+
CFO Approval
+
CEO Approval
---
# Competitive Quotes
Required for purchases above ₱50,000.
Minimum:
Three vendor quotations.
Exceptions require CFO approval.
---
# Vendor Onboarding
Vendors must provide:
- Business Registration
- Tax Documentation
- Banking Information
- Security Assessment (if applicable)
Refer to:
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
---
# Reimbursements
Employees shall submit:
- Receipts
- Business justification
- Expense report
within 30 days.
---
# Corporate Credit Cards
Authorized cardholders shall:
- Use cards for business expenses only
- Submit monthly reconciliation
---
# Travel Expenses
Covered expenses may include:
- Airfare
- Lodging
- Ground Transportation
- Meals
subject to approved limits.
---
# Financial Controls
The company follows segregation of duties.
No employee shall:
- Approve
- Process
- Reconcile
the same transaction.
---
# Audit Rights
The Finance Department may audit:
- Expense reports
- Procurement activities
- Vendor transactions
at any time.
---
# Fraud Reporting
Suspected fraud shall be reported immediately.
Reports may be made confidentially
through Human Resources or Finance.
Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
