# Code of Conduct
Policy ID: OD-POL-002
---
# Purpose
To establish behavioral expectations for all employees.
---
# Professional Conduct
Employees shall:
- Treat colleagues respectfully
- Maintain professionalism
- Avoid abusive language
- Support diversity and inclusion
---
# Workplace Harassment
Harassment is prohibited.
Examples include:
- Verbal abuse
- Bullying
- Intimidation
- Unwanted advances
- Discriminatory behavior
Violations may result in disciplinary action.
---
# Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose:
- Outside employment
- Financial interests
- Vendor relationships
Failure to disclose conflicts may lead to disciplinary action.
---
# Confidentiality
Employees shall not disclose:
- Financial information
- Customer information
- Internal business strategies
- Security procedures
See:
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
---
# Social Media Conduct
Employees may not:
- Present personal opinions as company positions
- Reveal confidential information
- Damage the company's reputation
---
# Ethical Standards
Employees shall:
- Follow laws and regulations
- Avoid bribery
- Avoid corruption
- Report unethical conduct
---
# Reporting Concerns
Concerns may be reported to:
- Direct manager
- Human Resources
- Executive Office
Retaliation against reporters is prohibited.
