# Information Technology Policy
Policy ID: OD-POL-006
Owner: Chief Technology Officer
Version: 1.0
---
# Purpose
This policy establishes standards for the acceptable use,
management, and protection of Othellow Digital technology assets.
---
# Scope
This policy applies to:
- Employees
- Contractors
- Interns
- Third-Party Consultants
who access company technology resources.
---
# Technology Assets
Technology assets include:
- Laptops
- Mobile devices
- Cloud services
- Software licenses
- Company email accounts
- Collaboration platforms
- Network infrastructure
---
# Asset Ownership
All company-issued technology assets remain
the property of Othellow Digital.
Employees shall return all equipment
upon separation from employment.
Refer to:
OD-POL-003 Human Resources Policy
---
# Acceptable Use
Employees may use company systems
for limited personal activities provided such use:
- Does not interfere with work
- Does not violate laws
- Does not create security risks
- Does not consume excessive resources
---
# Prohibited Activities
Employees shall not:
- Install unauthorized software
- Download pirated content
- Disable security tools
- Circumvent access controls
- Share company credentials
---
# Software Installation
Only approved software may be installed.
Software requests shall be submitted
through the IT Service Desk.
Approvals required:
- Employee Manager
- IT Department
Applications handling confidential data
require Information Security review.
Refer to:
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
---
# Email Usage
Corporate email shall be used
for legitimate business purposes.
Employees shall not:
- Send offensive material
- Send spam
- Conduct personal businesses
- Forward confidential information externally
---
# Cloud Services
Only approved cloud platforms may be used.
Approved services include:
- Microsoft 365
- SharePoint
- Teams
- Approved AI platforms
Unapproved cloud storage services
are prohibited.
---
# Device Standards
All company devices shall:
- Use endpoint protection software
- Enable disk encryption
- Enable automatic updates
- Use MFA where supported
---
# Data Backup
Business-critical data shall be backed up
according to the Business Continuity Policy.
Refer to:
OD-POL-014 Business Continuity Policy
---
# Technical Support
Support requests shall be submitted
through the approved ticketing platform.
Priority Levels:
P1 - Critical
P2 - High
P3 - Medium
P4 - Low
---
# Account Deactivation
Accounts shall be disabled within:
- 4 hours for involuntary separation
- 24 hours for voluntary separation
Refer to:
OD-POL-003 Human Resources Policy
OD-POL-007 Information Security Policy
